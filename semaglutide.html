Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist that works with the body's natural appetite-regulating processes. It may help reduce hunger, increase feelings of fullness, and support weight management alongside balanced nutrition, physical activity, and healthy lifestyle choices.

Semaglutide mimics the glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) hormone released after eating. It signals satiety centers in the brain to reduce cravings, slows gastric emptying to extend fullness after meals, and supports metabolic regulation to maintain sustainable weight management over time.

Important Safety Information

Compounded Medications Disclosure

Compounded medications are customized pharmaceutical products prepared by state-licensed 503A or FDA-registered 503B outsourcing compounding facilities upon receipt of a valid prescription. Compounded medications are not evaluated or approved by the FDA for safety, effectiveness, or quality.

GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Indications

Semaglutide is prescribed for chronic weight management in adults with an initial body mass index (BMI) of 30 kg/m² or greater (obesity) or 27 kg/m² or greater (overweight) in the presence of at least one weight-related comorbid condition.

Contraindications

Do not use Semaglutide if you or any of your family members have ever had Medullary Thyroid Carcinoma (MTC), or if you have Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2), or if you are pregnant or plan to become pregnant.

Potential Side Effects

Common side effects include nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal discomfort, headache, and fatigue. Consult your licensed provider immediately if you experience severe persistent abdominal pain.
